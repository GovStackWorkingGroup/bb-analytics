# 11. Internal Workflows and Data Lifecycle (Appendix)

Appendix — the end-to-end data lifecycle: ingestion and harmonisation, reconciliation and sovereign decision, and aggregation and proactive orchestration.

{% hint style="info" %}
🔄 **Three stages, ingestion to insight:** harmonise, reconcile, orchestrate.
{% endhint %}

This section outlines the end-to-end data lifecycle, illustrating how the Analytics BB interacts with adjacent GovStack components to reconcile data and orchestrate proactive workflows securely.

## 11.1 Ingestion and Harmonisation Pipeline

* **Event capture.** Change Data Capture (CDC) mechanisms or scheduled/file feeds detect new or changed data in upstream systems.
* **Pseudonymisation (where applicable).** Before data reaches the analytical perimeter, the ingestion/registry layer and the Information Mediator apply irreversible cryptographic hashing to any PII. For climate datasets, which are typically non-personal, this step handles sensitive or licensed-source restrictions instead.
* **Silver-layer harmonisation.** The Analytics BB receives the payload via the API intake interface, validates and cleans it, and merges it into the Golden Record and estimate tables, updating the historical state.

## 11.2 Reconciliation and Sovereign Decision

* **Multi-source assembly.** For each analytical unit, every available source is stored as a separate estimate, each with its own value, confidence and known limitations.
* **Gap analysis.** The Building Block computes the discrepancy per unit against the agreed threshold; units that agree are surfaced as low-risk, units that diverge are queued for adjudication.
* **Governed human review.** A steward or officer designates the authoritative source (or a documented blend) and records a rationale; the decision is written append-only with identity and timestamp, and is reversible.

## 11.3 Aggregation and Proactive Orchestration

* **Gold-layer modelling.** The orchestrator runs batch ELT pipelines to transform validated Silver records into Gold-layer data marts (e.g. a validated inventory or indicator mart).
* **Scoring and monitoring.** MLOps microservices evaluate Gold tables against models or policy matrices; reconciliation monitors re-flag units whose figures shift.
* **Dispatch and notify.** When a condition is met, the proactive-trigger module dispatches a standardised event to the Workflow BB, or a webhook to consuming systems when data is published or promoted.
